MeshCore Cardputer

Standalone MeshCore chat for the M5Stack Cardputer-Adv + Cap LoRa-1262 · vVERSION

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial).
  2. Plug in the Cardputer-Adv with a USB-C data cable and switch it on.
  3. Press Install and pick the Cardputer's port (usually “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”).
  4. When it's done, switch the Cardputer off and on (don't hold G0) and follow the setup on screen.
This browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs an HTTPS page.

Updating? Don't erase, and your contacts, channels and messages are kept. Erasing gives the device a new identity. If the port doesn't show up, hold G0 while switching on to enter download mode.
