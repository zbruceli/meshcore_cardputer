MeshCore chat for the **M5Stack Cardputer-Adv + Cap LoRa-1262**: screen and keyboard, no phone needed.

## New

- **Power off.** Hold **G0** for 3 seconds to switch off; press G0 to turn it back on. The clock keeps running while it's off.
- **Battery warnings.** A beep and a message at 10 % and 5 %. When the battery is empty, it saves your data and switches itself off instead of browning out.
- **Map.** On **3 RPT**, press `M` to see every contact with a known location around you, north up.
  - Repeaters are squares and everyone else is a ring. Bright means heard in the last day.
  - `;` `.` step through them from nearest to farthest, showing name, distance and direction.
  - `,` `/` zoom from 500 m to 200 km, and `Enter` opens the contact.
- **GPS pin in the status bar** while the GPS is fully on (location mode or the wardrive logger). It's filled once there's a fix.
- **Test tab, new pages** (`6 TST`, turn it on in SET):
  - **Run**, for tests that transmit. They're few and spaced out, and never on Public.
    - **Hear me:** 3 zero-hop discoveries, showing how many repeaters answer and how well they hear you.
    - **Echo #test:** 3 messages on `#test`, counting the repeats of each.
    - **Trace:** to a repeater and back, with the SNR at every hop.
    - **SDR burst:** 10 zero-hop adverts, for measuring your transmitter.
  - **Log**, a wardrive logger. It records which repeaters you hear where, to an SD card (FAT32), as CSV.
    - It only listens unless you turn on an occasional zero-hop ping (`D`).
    - The live view shows the best repeaters nearby, with SNR and link margin.
    - `scripts/wardrive_kml.py` turns the files into a Google Earth map.

## Good to know

- While the GPS is awake, the radio is about 4 dB less sensitive (measured). Normal GPS use only wakes it for a few seconds at a time. The wardrive logger keeps it on, so weak repeaters may drop out of a log.
- An SD card is needed only for the wardrive logger. Cards over 32 GB usually need formatting as FAT32 on a computer first.

## Upgrading

Flash over v0.3.x or v0.4.x without erasing. Your identity, contacts, channels, messages and settings are kept.

- The [web installer](https://zbruceli.github.io/meshcore_cardputer/) also keeps the phone app pairing.
- After M5Burner or esptool (one image at `0x0`), pair the phone app again: on your phone, forget `MeshCore-<name>`, then connect with the PIN from 5 SET.

Only the Cardputer-**Adv** with the Cap LoRa-1262 is supported, at 868–923 MHz.
