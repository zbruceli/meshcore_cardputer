MeshCore chat for the **M5Stack Cardputer-Adv + Cap LoRa-1262**: screen and keyboard, no phone needed.

## New in v0.4.1

- **The phone app's GPS switch works.** Position Settings in the MeshCore app now shows GPS on/off instead of "Not Supported".
  - App **off** is GPS off.
  - App **on** is the Cardputer's `clock` or `location` mode. Coming from off, it picks `location`.
  - All three modes remain available on the device in SET > **GPS**.

Coming from v0.3.x? See the [v0.4.0 notes](https://github.com/zbruceli/meshcore_cardputer/releases/tag/v0.4.0) for GPS location, quick replies, colour schemes and the test tab.

## Upgrading

Flash over v0.3.x or v0.4.0 without erasing, using M5Burner, the [web installer](https://zbruceli.github.io/meshcore_cardputer/) or esptool at `0x0`. Your identity, contacts, channels, messages and settings are kept.

**After upgrading, pair Bluetooth again:** on your phone, forget `MeshCore-<name>`, then connect with the PIN from SET.
